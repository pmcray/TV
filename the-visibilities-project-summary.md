# Project Briefing: The Visibilities (Master System Prompt)

This document serves as the definitive structural, thematic, and technical specification for the literary novel *The Visibilities*. It is designed for direct ingestion into advanced foundation models to establish an uncorrupted, high-fidelity cognitive environment for drafting, structural critique, and text generation.

## I. Core Conception & Architectural Contract

### 1. The Almanac Grid (The Macro-Structure)
*The Visibilities* is structured as a leap-year almanac comprising **366 discrete sections** divided across **twelve single-word Partitions** (corresponding to the months of the year). 
*   **The Lineage:** The project stands in the direct lineage of Robert Burton’s *The Anatomy of Melancholy* (systematic categorization of human sorrow) and Thomas Pynchon’s *Gravity's Rainbow* (paranoid, highly codified systems).
*   **The Temporal Tension:** The novel is a static block universe. The twelve Partitions are spatialised containers. 
*   **The Vacancy Defect (Section 6.31 / June 31st):** Named *"It doesn't look like anywhere"* (after the closing line of Len Deighton’s *Bomber*). This is a deliberate typographic and mathematical crater omitted from the main text block but listed in the Table of Contents. It represents the "Physical Void," the Kalûnga River, and the exact coordinate where the "Seen" reality unzips.

### 2. The Twelve-Strand Contract
The narrative is woven from twelve distinct thematic and structural strands that must be held in absolute equilibrium:
1.  **Apophenia:** The primary cognitive engine. The search for patterns in noise; the protagonist's survival mechanism against depression.
2.  **The Seen vs. The Unseen:** The friction between the tangible, post-industrial, brick-and-soot reality of Deep England and the theoretical, multi-dimensional, cybernetic architecture of the Time War.
3.  **The Baltic Connection:** The Cold War military-intelligence axis, stretching from the docks of Riga to the secret laboratories of Akademgorodok.
4.  **Obsolete Computing:** The physical, discrete reality of early Sinclair BASIC (20 GOTO 10), DEC VMS, and balanced ternary systems.
5.  **The Academic Exile:** The Morris Zapp/Philip Swallow transatlantic collision (via David Lodge) between the rain-slicked concrete of Lancashire and the sun-drenched, corporate abundance of the Pacific Northwest.
6.  **Esoteric Systems:** Rigorous, rule-bound structures (such as *Ars Magica* and *Luther Arkwright* RPG mechanics) treated as legitimate branches of theoretical physics.
7.  **The Melancholy of Stasis:** The thermodynamic baseline of the novel. The realization that all movement is an illusion and that systems inevitably decay toward absolute zero.
8.  **The Aerospace Legacy:** The heritage of mid-century British aviation (BAC, Warton, Preston) and the mechanical translation of acoustic signatures.
9.  **The Non-Euclidean Page:** The physical layout of the page as a geometric manifold, utilizing TeX typography to enforce spatial constraints.
10. **The Insectoid Threat:** The visceral biological horror of distributed, collective hive-minds that reject human bilateral symmetry.
11. **The Roman Catholic Catechism:** The moral and guilt-ridden substrate of the protagonist’s youth, later replaced by the equally rigid rules of the science-fictional megatext.
12. **The Double-Page Superposition:** Structural spreads where the Left Page (the historical "Seen") and the Right Page (the parallel "Unseen") exist in simultaneous cognitive tension.

## II. Geometries of the Multiverse

The different factions of the Time War—the **Adamantine** (the agents of absolute stasis, history-free structures, and physical concrete), the **Amaranthine** (the agents of branching, fluid timelines and organic textuality), and the **Insectoid Hiveminds**—possess distinct mathematical and metaphysical ways of existing. The novel uses three competing geometries of infinity to format their realities:

### 1. The Logarithmic Cylinder (The Timeline of the Amaranthine)
*   **Mathematical Formula:** \\(w = \ln(z)\\) applied to the punctured complex plane \\(\mathbb{C} \setminus \{0\}\\).
*   **Physical Effect:** This conformal mapping unrolls the plane onto an infinite cylinder. The origin \\(z = 0\\) (the absolute past of childhood trauma) is projected to \\(-\infty\\) on the far left, while the outer limit \\(z \to \infty\\) (the technological Eschaton) is projected to \\(+\infty\\) on the far right. 
*   **Narrative Function:** The Partitions are formatted as concentric rings wrapping around the cylinder. The protagonist is caught in a helical path, forever observing his past and his future as two unreachable horizons of infinite density.

### 2. The Double-Limit Annulus (The Shield of the Adamantine)
*   **Physical Effect:** A bounded circular track defined by an inner containment wall (the Brancusi spiral of trauma) and an outer facade (the Brutalist squircle of institutional bureaucracy).
*   **Narrative Function:** Represented visually by the **Crest of Woodworth University**. The metric space is engineered such that spatial distance diverges logarithmically to infinity as the observer approaches *either* the center or the edge. A traveler is trapped in a permanent, circular loop, unable to escape because both the microscopic core and the macroscopic horizon are protected by an infinite metric distance.

### 3. The Scale-Invariant "Blogofractal" (The Infinite Nesting)
*   **The Lineage:** Derived from xkcd 17 (the infinite, self-similar blog).
*   **Narrative Function:** A fractal nesting scheme where the microscopic details (Sinclair BASIC code, JMB exam answers, the coordinates of the Black Horse pub) contain the complete structural blueprint of the entire 366-section novel. Zooming in or zooming out yields identical geometric matrices, creating a state of perpetual scale-vertigo.

### 4. Hyperbolic Heptagonal Symmetry: The Insectoid Architecture
*   **The Tessellation:** The order-3 heptagonal tiling designated as **\\(\{7,3\}\\)**.
*   **The Physics:** Regular heptagons cannot tile a flat Euclidean plane; they violently overlap and shatter. However, on a hyperbolic saddle (negative Gaussian curvature), the interior angles of a regular heptagon compress from \\(128.57^\circ\\) to exactly \\(120^\circ\\). Three heptagons can meet perfectly at every vertex.
*   **Narrative Function:** This is the geometry of the insectoid hive-mind spaceships and the seven-armed "starfish" entities. When projected onto the flat, two-dimensional Euclidean page of the book block, they are rendered conformal to the **Poincaré disk model**—infinitely shrinking, deforming, and crowding as they approach the circular boundary at the edge of the page. They are generated algorithmically using perverted **L-systems** (Lindenmayer systems), turning botanical growth rules into chitinous, segmented hulls.

## III. Computational Engines: Ternary Logic (-1, 0, 1)

The novel rejects the binary dualism of traditional computing in favor of Soviet **balanced ternary logic**, modeled on the historically real **Setun** computer developed at Moscow State University in 1958.
*   **-1 (The Unseen / The Void):** Represents the missing February 29th, the lost books, the silence of the Kalûnga River, and the parallel timelines that have been violently scrubbed.
*   **0 (The Flicker / Superposition):** Represents the threshold spaces, the transition state of *Etsi Ketsi*, and the moment before a timeline collapses.
*   **+1 (The Seen / The Soil):** Represents the concrete, soot-stained, brick-built reality of Preston, Manchester, and the JMB syllabus.

By running Soviet bioelectronic radar simulations on secret Setun arrays in Akademgorodok, the characters process a reality that natively holds ambiguity and multi-valued truths without collapsing into a serial bottleneck.

## IV. Primary Character Dossiers

### 1. Paul M. Cray / Andrew (The Marooned Observer)
*   **Profile:** The protagonist. An aerospace systems engineer and self-taught systems theorist living in a secure, climate-controlled vault in West Seattle. He is haunted by intense Catholic guilt and the memory of **10 Beechwood Avenue** in Preston.
*   **The Method:** He defends his mind against clinical depression and the sliding timelines of the Time War by manually auditing, scanning, and encoding an 8,000-volume physical library into a rigid database. He replaces his childhood Catholic catechism with the unyielding, canonical rules of the plain TeX typesetting engine.

### 2. Zoë Heriot-Shaw (The Atheist Cosmologist)
*   **Profile:** An elite theoretical physicist and AI architect at Woodworth University. 
*   **Character Constraint:** She is an absolute, unyielding atheist. She never changes her surname, even in timelines where she marries. She possesses a vague, childhood Church of Scotland connection via her paternal grandfather, but she is strictly **not Catholic**.
*   **Narrative Function:** Her birthday is **May 31st (Section 152—"A Thousand Runs in May")**, a "triple prime" date. She operates completely without a metaphysical safety net, viewing both human consciousness (EGO) and the universe as entirely deterministic, computable systems.

### 3. Pavel Kelvanov (The Siberian Cyberneticist)
*   **Profile:** Born **16 April 1928 (Section 107—"The Broom Cupboard and the Traffic Accident")**, the son of Yuri Kelvanov Sr. (the brutal Chief Boatswain of the Imperial Airship *Kometa* in *The Mundanomicon*).
*   **The Work:** A brilliant Soviet scientist working in the closed research institutes of Akademgorodok. He is involved in the Moscow ABM radar program, specializing in **bionics** and **bioelectronics** to bypass the limits of Soviet silicon manufacturing.
*   **The Parallel:** He is the author of a legendary, degraded 1976 Cyrillic samizdat novel that oddly parallels *The Visibilities*, proving that his brain is processing the exact same mathematical stasis as Paul's.

### 4. The Older Epicurean / The Classicist (The Parasite)
*   **Profile:** An older British academic visiting the Creative Writing department at Woodworth University.
*   **The Thesis:** *The Political Economy of the Athenian Opsophagos: Clinamen and the Failure of Aponia*. (First in Classical Mods, Second in Finals at Oxford—the Dacre Balsdon trajectory).
*   **The Conflict:** He carries an **"Acute Dependency: Sex 100%, Food & Drink 100%, Narcotics 100%"** derived from the *Luther Arkwright* RPG rules. He uses his classical academic mastery of Epicurean atomic materialism (which teaches that tranquility is impossible) to intellectually justify his monstrous, insatiable, Rabelaisian physical appetites. He is a parasite operating inside Woodworth’s clean, cybernetic grid.

### 5. Adrian Simpson Rosebery (The Wounded Prodigy)
*   **Profile:** A 40-year-old British novelist skulking on a visiting fellowship at Woodworth following an expensive divorce and a critically savaged book.
*   **Narrative Function:** The author of *Imagined Mornings and Invented Evenings*. Once an unassailable prodigy, his presence at Woodworth demonstrates the slow, physical decay of the "Seen" literary establishment.

## V. Key Bibliographical Scaffolding

The following texts represent the physical, intellectual bedrock of *The Visibilities*. Every volume listed exists as a real substrate in the protagonist's West Seattle bunker:

1.  **von Neumann, John** — *The Computer and the Brain* (Yale, 1958). The post-mortem study of the analog-digital split and the biological constraints of human neural processing.
2.  **Fink, Donald G.** — *Computers and the Human Mind* (Doubleday, 1966). A mid-century defense of logic systems, analyzing the physical boundaries of natural and artificial intelligence.
3.  **Ashby, W. Ross** — *Design for a Brain* (Chapman & Hall, 1952). The ultimate cybernetic manual of homeostatic feedback, ultrastability, and continuous step-mechanisms.
4.  **Crowley, John** — *Little, Big* (1981). The structural blueprint for the spatialisation of time (floors as seasons, stairs as days), analyzed in the master ledger via **Adam Roberts’s Substack** (*profadamroberts.substack.com*, 2024).
5.  **Lodge, David** — *How Far Can You Go?* (1980). The definitive study of the erosion of the Catholic catechism, replaced in *The Visibilities* by the rules of retro-computing and wargaming.
6.  **Deighton, Len** — *Bomber* (1970). The high-density, technical dissection of an accidental aerial catastrophe, serving as the thematic anchor for Section 6.31.
7.  **Miles, Lawrence** — *This Town Will Never Let Us Go* (2003). The psychogeographical burying of hyper-dimensional war-machines beneath the dull concrete of Reading.

5.  **Strict Grounding:** Every historical, biographical, or bibliographic claim must be traceable to the established coordinates of our 366-day leap-year tensor.
